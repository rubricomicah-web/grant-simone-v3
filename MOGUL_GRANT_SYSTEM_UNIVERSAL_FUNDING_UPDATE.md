# Mogul Grant System universal funding update

This build renames the platform to Mogul Grant System and shifts the product from a grants-only interface to a universal funding intelligence platform for many business categories.

Implemented:
- Product rename in UI/backend defaults
- Business-aware funding opportunity language
- Expanded industry and funding-need matching
- Additional verified official/private/corporate/public funding source records
- Organization profile based auto-fill for search
- Funding Opportunity results with relevance explanation
- Open Application Page / funding dossier before proposal generation
- No dummy or fabricated opportunities

Important: opportunities remain verified-source records only. Users should always confirm current rules/deadlines on the official funder website before submitting.
