# Mogul Grant System Plan Features

All signups go through Stripe Checkout first. The account remains locked until payment succeeds.

## Individual

### Starter - $19/mo
- Personal grant and assistance search
- Scholarships, housing, career training, utility, emergency aid
- Save grants
- Email and in-platform grant alerts
- Deadline reminders
- 5 AI application assists per month
- Personal dashboard

Limits: no autonomous workflows, no premium private intelligence, no advanced scoring.

### Pro - $49/mo
- Everything in Starter
- Unlimited AI proposal/application narratives
- Private, foundation, corporate, scholarship, and personal aid opportunities
- AI competitiveness scoring
- Uploaded document memory
- Proposal/application history
- Advanced filters and workflow tracking

Limits: no autonomous submission agent, no white-label portal.

### Elite - $99/mo
- Everything in Pro
- Autonomous workflows with approval gate
- AI application preparation
- Premium verified opportunities
- AI review and compliance checker
- Smart deadline monitoring
- AI funding strategist
- Priority processing and support
